# Support & FAQ

## Getting started

**What is Luna’s World?**

A free community app for cat lovers in the Arabic-speaking world. Share posts and photos, chat, find or list cats for adoption, report missing cats, enter cat competitions, keep your cats’ health records, and get reminders and safety guides, in Arabic and English.

**Which devices does it work on?**

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. There is no Android or web version at the moment.

**Is it free?**

Yes. There are no ads and no payments in the app.

**How do I sign up or sign in?**

Use your email and a password, or Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with Google. Choose a username and you’re in.

**How old do I need to be?**

At least 13. If you’re under 18, please use the app with a parent or guardian’s approval.

**How do I switch between Arabic and English, or light and dark mode?**

Open the menu (☰). You’ll find the Language and Appearance rows there.

**I forgot my password.**

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password” and we’ll email you a reset link. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that button to sign in again; there’s no separate password.

## Your cats and their health

**How do I add my cat?**

Open your profile or the Health tab and tap the add button. Add a name, a photo, and basic details such as breed, sex and age or birth date. You can add as many cats as you have.

**What can I keep in a cat’s health record?**

Weight over time, vaccines and preventive care, medicines and dose schedules, vet visits, symptoms with photos, notes, attached documents, an optional microchip number, and a “what the vet needs to know” card you can show at the clinic.

**Who can see my cat’s health records?**

Only you. Health records are private and are never shown on your public profile. Only the cat’s basic card (name, photo, breed) can be seen by others.

**How do reminders work?**

Add a medicine, vaccine, appointment or birthday and choose when to be reminded. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself, so they work without internet. Make sure notifications are allowed for Luna’s World in your iPhone’s Settings.

**My reminder didn’t go off.**

Check that notifications are on for the app, that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn’t silencing it, and that your phone’s date and time are correct. Reminders are a helpful nudge but can fail, so for a medicine your cat must not miss, please also set your own alarm.

**What is the home-screen widget?**

A small widget that shows your cat’s next dose, vaccine or vet visit. Touch and hold your home screen, tap +, and pick Luna’s World. On the lock screen the details are hidden for privacy.

**How do I record a symptom or a vet visit?**

Open your cat in the Health tab, choose Symptoms or Vet visits, and tap add. You can attach photos and notes, and everything stays private to you.

## Safety and the symptom checker

**What is the Safety tab?**

A place for quick, practical guidance: a symptom checker, lists of foods, plants and products that are harmful to cats, and first-aid style tips. The guides are written to be read fast when you’re worried.

**Can the symptom checker diagnose my cat?**

No. It asks a few questions and gives a general sense of how urgent things may be. It can be wrong, and “low urgency” doesn’t mean your cat is fine. It never replaces a vet. If you’re worried, call a vet now.

**Is the symptom checker private?**

Yes. Your answers are worked out on your phone. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to save the result to your cat’s record.

**My cat ate something toxic. What do I do?**

Call your vet or the nearest emergency vet immediately, and don’t wait for symptoms. Don’t make your cat vomit unless a vet tells you to. Take the packaging or a photo of what was eaten. The Safety tab’s guides can help you recognise common dangers, but they are not a replacement for professional help.

## Community, adoption and missing cats

**How do I list a cat for adoption?**

Open the Adoption tab and tap the add button. Add photos, the cat’s name, breed, age, your city and a contact number, then post. Everyone browsing adoption in that city can see it.

**Is my phone number visible?**

If you type a number in a listing, yes: every signed-in user can see it. Only include a number you’re happy to share, and be careful with anyone who asks for money in advance.

**How do I report a missing cat?**

Open the Missing Cats tab, add a photo and details (breed, city, when and where she was last seen, how to reach you) and post. People in that city can see it, and users who posted in the same city are notified.

**Can I make a poster for a missing cat?**

Yes. From your missing-cat report you can create a poster in Arabic and English, then save it or share it (for example on WhatsApp). The poster is made on your phone.

**My cat was found or adopted. What now?**

Open your listing or report and mark it as adopted or found instead of deleting it. It moves out of the active list and keeps a record of the happy ending. You can reactivate it from the same place.

**Does Luna’s World check adopters or listings?**

No. We’re not a party to adoptions and can’t verify people. Meet in a public place, ask questions, check the cat with a vet, and never send money before meeting. If something looks wrong, report the listing.

**How accurate is the breed detector?**

It’s a fun estimate from a photo, made on your phone, not an official or veterinary identification. If it isn’t confident, it says no cat was detected instead of guessing.

**What are competitions?**

Fun cat competitions: enter a photo or video of your cat and vote for others. Votes must be genuine; bought or automated votes can be removed.

**Can I message other users or make a group?**

Yes. Open someone’s profile and tap the message icon for a one-to-one chat, or start a group chat from the Messages tab. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.

**How do I save a post for later?**

Tap the bookmark icon on any post. Your saved posts are collected under Bookmarks in the menu.

**Can I search for a breed or a post?**

Yes. The feed, adoption and missing-cat tabs each have a search bar for breeds, names and keywords, as well as city filters.

## Safety, blocking and reporting

**How do I block or report someone?**

On a profile, post or comment, tap the ••• menu and choose Block or Report. Blocking hides their posts, listings and comments from you and stops them contacting you. They aren’t told you blocked them. Manage your block list in Settings → Blocked accounts.

**What happens after I report something?**

Our team reviews it and can remove content, warn, or suspend accounts. The person you reported never sees who reported them. If we remove someone’s content, we tell them the reason.

**I think my account was suspended or my post removed by mistake.**

Email support@lsquared.sa from your account’s email address with a short description and we’ll take another look.

**Someone is sharing my photos or personal information.**

Report the post or profile from the ••• menu and then email support@lsquared.sa with a link or screenshot. We take this seriously.

## Notifications

**What notifications will I get?**

New followers, likes, comments, messages, missing-cat reports in a city where you’ve also posted, and the reminders you set. Tapping one opens the right post or chat.

**How do I turn notifications off or choose which ones I get?**

In the app: Settings → Notification settings. For everything at once, use your iPhone’s Settings → Notifications → Luna’s World.

**I’m not getting notifications.**

Check that notifications are allowed in your iPhone’s Settings, that Focus mode isn’t hiding them, and that you’re signed in. If it still doesn’t work, sign out and back in, or email us.

## Privacy and your account

**Do you share my exact location?**

No. The app never reads your GPS location. Listings are organised by a city that you choose yourself.

**Do you sell my data or show ads?**

No. There are no ads, we don’t sell your data, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

**Can I turn off analytics?**

Yes. Go to Settings → “Help improve the app” and switch it off. We use basic anonymous usage and performance information to fix bugs and make the app faster. Crash reports are always sent so we can fix crashes, and they never include your posts or messages.

**How do I edit or delete my posts and listings?**

Open the item from your profile and use the edit or delete option. Deleting a listing or report also permanently removes its photos.

**How do I delete my account?**

Open the menu (☰) → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. This removes your profile, posts, photos, cats and health records, listings and more. You don’t need to contact us. The full list of what is removed and what may remain (for example, messages already sent to other people, and short-lived backups) is in our Privacy Policy.

**I want a copy of my data, or want a message removed.**

Email support@lsquared.sa from the email address on your account and we’ll help. We aim to reply within 30 days.

**Where is my data stored?**

On Google Cloud servers in the United States. See the Privacy Policy for details on international transfers.

## Troubleshooting and contact

**The app is slow, stuck or crashing.**

Update to the latest version from the App Store, force-close and reopen the app, and check your internet connection. If it keeps happening, email us with your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing. Crash reports are sent to us automatically.

**My photo or video won’t upload.**

Check your connection and that the app is allowed to use your camera or photos in iPhone Settings. Videos can be up to two minutes; shorter videos upload faster.

**Who makes Luna’s World, and how do I contact you?**

Luna’s World is designed and built by LSquared, an iOS app design and development studio in Saudi Arabia. Email support@lsquared.sa; we usually reply within a day or two.
