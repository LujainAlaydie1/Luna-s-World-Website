# Privacy Policy

*Last updated 1 November 2026*

## 1. Who we are

Luna’s World (“the App”) is built and run by **L Squared Est.** (“LSquared,” “we,” “us”), based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We are the party that decides how your personal data is used in the App and on **lunasworld.lsquared.sa** (“the Website”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

## 2. The short version

- We collect only what the App’s features need to work.
- We **do not sell** your data, we show **no ads**, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The App does not read your advertising identifier.
- Your cats’ **health records are private**: only you can see them.
- Your profile, posts, comments, listings and competition entries are **public inside the App**. If you put a phone number in a listing, every signed-in user can see it.
- You can **delete your account** yourself, at any time, from Settings.

## 3. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account and profile:** your email address, a username, your name, an optional bio, and optional profile and cover photos. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email they share with us (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one). Your password is handled by Google’s Firebase Authentication; we never see it or store it in readable form.
- **Posts and community content:** posts, questions, blog posts, comments, and the photos and short videos (up to two minutes) you attach, from your camera or photo library.
- **Listings:** adoption, mating and missing-cat listings, with photos, the cat’s details, your city, and the **contact phone number** you type into the form.
- **Your cats:** a profile for each cat (name, breed, sex, age or birth date, photos) and, in the Health tab, its **health records**: weight, vaccines and preventive care, medicines and dose schedules, vet visits, symptoms and photos, notes, attached documents, an optional microchip number, and the “what the vet needs to know” card. A cat’s basic card can appear on your public profile; the health records never do.
- **Competitions:** your entry (photo or video, caption, your cat’s name) and the votes you cast.
- **Messages:** the messages, shared posts and photos you send in one-to-one and group chats.
- **Social and safety actions:** who you follow, posts you save, people you block, and reports you file (what and whom you reported, and the reason you give).
- **Messages to support:** if you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.

### Information collected automatically

- **Push notification address:** if you allow notifications, a device token issued by Apple and Firebase Cloud Messaging. It is kept in a private area of your account and is used only to deliver notifications to you.
- **Usage analytics and performance:** basic, anonymous events such as which main screen was opened, that a post or a symptom check was completed, and how fast the App starts. Events never contain names, messages, captions, photos, emails, phone numbers or account IDs. This is **on by default and you can switch it off** in Settings → “Help improve the app”; switching it off stops usage and performance collection and clears the analytics data stored on your device.
- **Crash reports:** when the App crashes we receive technical details (the error type, the device model, the iOS and App versions) so we can fix it. These do not include the content of your posts or messages. Crash reports are always sent and are not controlled by the analytics switch.
- **Your language and display choices** are stored on your device.

### What we do not collect

- We do **not** collect your device’s location. Cities in listings are chosen by you from a list.
- We do **not** read your contacts, your whole photo library (you pick photos one by one through the system picker), or your advertising identifier.
- We do **not** collect payment card details. The App has no payments at the moment.

## 4. Permissions the App may ask for

- **Camera and microphone** — to take photos and record short videos (the microphone records the sound in the video). Only when you use those buttons.
- **Photo library** — to choose photos and videos, and to save an image (for example a lost-cat poster) when you ask us to.
- **Notifications** — for activity alerts and for the medicine, vaccine and appointment reminders you set.

You can change any of these at any time in your iPhone’s Settings. The App keeps working without them, only the matching feature is unavailable.

## 5. Features that run on your device

- **Breed detector:** the photo is analysed on your device by a model inside the App and is **not uploaded** unless you separately post or save it.
- **Symptom checker:** your answers are worked out on your device. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to save the result to your cat’s record or share the summary yourself. The last check’s name and result are remembered on your device so the Safety tab can show it.
- **Reminders:** medicine, vaccine, appointment and birthday reminders are scheduled **locally on your phone**. They are not sent through our servers.
- **Home-screen widget:** the next dose, vaccine or visit is shared between the App and the widget on your device. Details are hidden on the lock screen. Signing out or deleting your account clears the widget and the reminders from the phone.
- **Lost-cat poster:** the poster is made on your device. If your iPhone supports it, Apple’s on-device translation fills in the other language. A phone number you type is remembered on the device for convenience and cleared when you sign out.

## 6. How we use your information

- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To run the features you use: showing your public content, listings and competition entries, delivering messages, keeping your cats’ records, and sending notifications.
- To tell you about activity (new followers, likes, comments, messages) and about missing-cat reports in a city where you have also posted.
- To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our Terms, limiting spam and abuse, and suspending accounts when needed. If we remove your content we tell you why.
- To find and fix bugs, keep the App fast and reliable, and understand in general terms how it is used.
- To answer you when you contact support, and to meet legal obligations.

Our grounds for using your data are: your consent (for example when you create an account, allow a permission, or keep analytics on), what we need to provide the App to you, our legitimate interest in keeping the App safe and working, and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your photos, videos or messages for advertising or to train AI models.

## 7. Who can see what

- **Everyone in the App (signed-in users):** your username, name, profile and cover photos, bio, followers and following, your posts, comments, blog posts, listings (including the contact number you typed), competition entries, and the basic cards of your cats.
- **Only the people in a conversation:** messages in a one-to-one chat or group. Other users and the public never see them.
- **Only you:** your email address, your cats’ health records and documents, your bookmarks, your block list, your push address, and your drafts.
- **Our moderation team:** content that is reported, the reports themselves (including who filed them), and the records of moderation actions. Who you reported or blocked is never shown to the other person.

Things you share outside the App (for example a poster you send on WhatsApp, a post you share, or a screenshot someone takes) are out of our control.

## 8. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data for us under their own security and data-protection terms:

- **Google (Firebase and Google Cloud):** sign-in, our database, photo and video storage, server functions, push delivery, analytics, crash and performance reports, and remote on/off switches for features.
- **Apple:** Sign in with Apple, push notifications (APNs), the App Store, and on-device features like translation.
- **Google Sign-In:** if you choose to sign in with Google.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety, or to protect our rights, and in the event of a sale or restructuring of the business (we would tell you first and this policy would continue to apply). When you tap a phone number or WhatsApp link in a listing, you leave the App and that app’s own policy applies.

## 9. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers located in the **United States** and may be processed by Google in other countries. This means your data leaves Saudi Arabia. We only transfer personal data outside the Kingdom as the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law allows, relying on safeguards such as the contractual data-protection terms of our providers. You can ask us about these safeguards at any time.

## 10. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete things one by one (posts, comments, listings, cats), or delete the whole account:

> **To delete your account:** open the menu (☰) → Settings → **Delete account**, then confirm your identity. You do not need to contact us.

Deleting your account removes your profile, posts, comments, blog posts, photos and videos, your cats and all their health records and files, your listings, competition entries, follows, bookmarks, notifications and block list, and your push address. Your username becomes available again, and if you signed in with Apple we revoke the connection to your Apple ID. If the deletion is interrupted (for example the connection drops), it finishes the next time you open the App.

What can remain after deletion, and why:

- **Messages you sent** stay in the conversations you sent them to, because they are part of the other person’s conversation. They are no longer linked to a working profile. You can ask us to remove specific messages (see section 11).
- **Safety records:** reports filed about content or people, and records of moderation actions, are kept as long as needed for safety and legal reasons. If an account was suspended, we keep a minimal marker (the account ID) so that deleting and re-registering cannot be used to avoid the suspension.
- **Backups:** our database keeps point-in-time recovery data for 7 days and daily backups for 14 days. Deleted information disappears from them automatically after that period.
- **Anonymous analytics and crash data** are not linked to your account, so they are not removed with it.
- **Copies others made**, for example a shared post, a screenshot, or a poster you sent.

## 11. Your choices and rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, and similar laws where they apply to you, you have the right to know how your data is used, to access a copy of it, to correct it, to ask for it to be deleted, and to withdraw your consent. Most of this you can do yourself in the App:

- Edit your profile, posts, listings and cats, or delete them, at any time.
- Turn usage analytics off in Settings → “Help improve the app”.
- Turn notifications, camera, microphone and photo access on or off in your iPhone’s Settings; manage which activity alerts you get in Settings → Notification settings, and your blocked accounts in Settings → Blocked accounts.
- Block people and report content or users from the ••• menu.
- Delete your account from Settings (see section 10).

For anything else, such as a copy of your data, a correction we cannot make in the App, or removing specific messages, email [support@lsquared.sa](mailto:support@lsquared.sa) from the email address on your account. We may need to verify it is you, and we aim to reply within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

## 12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and Google encrypts it at rest. Our database rules decide who can read each piece of data (for example, health records are readable only by their owner), and we test those rules before changing them. Access to moderation tools is limited to our team. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device locked. If you find a security problem, please tell us at support@lsquared.sa.

## 13. Children

Luna’s World is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, please use the App with the knowledge and approval of a parent or guardian. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it. If you believe this has happened, email us.

## 14. This website

The Website uses **no cookies, no analytics and no advertising or third-party trackers**, and it loads no outside scripts or fonts. Your browser stores only your language and light/dark choice on your own device. If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you.

## 15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and for important changes we will also tell you in the App before they take effect. Using the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
